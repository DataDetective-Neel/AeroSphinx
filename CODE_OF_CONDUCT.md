# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be specific. This project debates numbers, methods and
evidence; it does not debate the worth of the people producing them.

## Expected

* Critique the work, not the author: quote the artefact, the line, the file.
* Say when you are unsure. "I could not reproduce this" is a complete and useful
  report.
* Assume a mistake before assuming bad faith; a wrong number is usually a bug.
* Accept a correction cleanly and record it, rather than defending the original.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
* Dismissing a contribution because of who made it.
* Publishing someone's private data or communications.
* Deliberately misleading claims about what the data shows.

## Scope and enforcement

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space. Reports go
to the maintainer in `CITATION.cff`; they are read, kept confidential, and
answered. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, at the
maintainer's discretion, according to severity and pattern.
