## 5. What the reproduced baseline actually shows

### 5.1 Dataset sizes

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| CPCB hourly rows read | 78,912 across 9 files |
| CPCB daily rows accepted | 2,979 (2016-01-01 to 2024-12-31) |
| Daily rows rejected by the 18-hour rule | 309 |
| AOD granules / distinct dates | 169 / 166 (2024-01-01 to 2024-06-18) |
| MERRA-2 granules / dates | 172 (2024-01-01 to 2024-06-20) |
| **Fused modelling rows** | **166** (2024-01-07 to 2024-06-21) |
| Training rows | 130 (2024-01-07 to 2024-05-16) |
| Test rows | 33 (2024-05-17 to 2024-06-18) |

The 9-year CPCB record collapses to a **166-row, 6-month, single-station**
dataset once AOD and MERRA-2 are required. That is the binding constraint on
every result below.

### 5.2 Headline metrics (test window, n = 33)

| Model | MAE | RMSE | R2 | AQI acc. | Bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RandomForest | 17.821 | 23.370 | -0.024 | 0.545 | +6.46 |
| XGBoost | 16.941 | 22.419 | 0.058 | 0.485 | +3.74 |
| **Weighted ensemble (0.6 RF / 0.4 XGB)** | **17.298** | 22.902 | **0.017** | 0.545 | +5.34 |
| Ensemble without persistence anchor | 20.118 | 26.177 | -0.284 | 0.515 | +8.91 |
| Persistence baseline | 19.918 | 24.186 | -0.096 | 0.424 | +0.00 |
| Climatology baseline | 35.866 | 44.322 | -2.681 | 0.242 | -35.85 |

Observed standard deviation in the test window is 23.10 ug/m3, so R2 = 0
corresponds to RMSE = 23.10. The ensemble's R2 of **0.017** means it explains
about 2% of the variance in daily PM2.5 over this window.

### 5.3 Three findings that reframe the research contribution

**(a) Fusion does not beat its own ground-only ablation.** On identical test
rows, `Ground_history_only` achieves **MAE 16.617**, better than the full
multisource ensemble's 17.298. `Satellite_AOD_only` (19.260) and
`Meteorology_only` (20.119) are both worse than persistence. **There is
currently no evidence that satellite or meteorological information adds
predictive value.** This is the central research question of the project, and
the current evidence does not support it. (D-007)

**(b) The improvement over persistence is not statistically significant.**
A paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the per-day absolute errors gives
**p = 0.1495**; a paired bootstrap of the MAE difference gives a 95% interval
of **[-6.47, +1.39]**, spanning zero. The ensemble's apparent advantage comes
substantially from the 0.4 persistence anchor — without it, MAE is 20.118,
worse than persistence. (D-006)

**(c) The model is a seasonal climatology, not a physical model.**
`month_sin` alone carries **37.93%** of the ensemble's normalised importance,
more than the three PM2.5 history features combined. (D-011)

### 5.4 The "forecast" is a retrospective estimation

The five-day recursive forecast is issued from 2024-06-16, but each forecast
day receives the **observed** AOD and MERRA-2 values for that day. The model
receives genuine satellite AOD for 2024-06-17 and 2024-06-18, and a
forward-filled AOD for 2024-06-19 through 2024-06-21. No external
meteorological forecast product exists in this repository, despite the
docstring asserting one.

| Horizon | Date | AOD received | Pixel support | T2M received |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2024-06-17 | 0.6911 (observed) | 6 px | 317.73 |
| 2 | 2024-06-18 | 0.8607 (observed) | 6 px | 317.22 |
| 3 | 2024-06-19 | 0.8607 (**forward-filled**) | — | 317.06 |
| 4 | 2024-06-20 | 0.8607 (**forward-filled**) | — | 314.25 |
| 5 | 2024-06-21 | 0.8607 (**forward-filled**) | — | 314.25 |

Under the plan's availability contract this is forbidden input for an
operational forecast. As shipped, **the pipeline cannot produce a valid
operational forecast at all** — which is itself the most important structural
finding of Phase 0, and precisely why the plan separates retrospective
estimation from operational forecasting. (D-002)

## 6. Schema inspection

### 6.1 CPCB

Nine annual hourly CSV exports, one file per year, 8,760-8,784 rows each
(2016, 2020 and 2024 are leap years). 25 columns; PM2.5 is
`PM2.5 (µg/m³)`. Timestamp format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`.

Data-quality observations:

- **Calendar gaps:** the accepted daily series has **123 gaps**, the longest
  being **25 days** (2017-10-09 to 2017-11-03). This is what makes the
  row-based lag defect material. (D-001)
- **Year-to-year heterogeneity:** the fraction of integer-valued hourly
  readings falls from 96.1% in 2016 to 0.3% in 2023, and mean absolute
  successive-hourly-difference falls from 19.3 to 9.9 ug/m3. Earlier years are
  raw hourly instrument values; later years are progressively smoothed or
  pre-aggregated. This is a genuine change in the *character* of the record
  across the 9 years, not merely noise, and it is not documented anywhere in
  the repository. It is INFERRED that upstream CPCB exports changed
  resolution or averaging over this period; the local files alone cannot
  confirm the cause.
- **Screening:** 407 invalid PM2.5 readings in 2016 alone, decreasing to 1 in
  2022, consistent with the [0, 500] ug/m3 range rule.
- Duplicated hourly rows across files: **0**.

### 6.2 INSAT-3D AOD

169 L2G granules, product `3DIMG_*_L2G_AOD_V02R00`, ISRO/SAC Ahmedabad.

```
AOD       (1, 551, 551) float32   _FillValue = -999.0
latitude  (551,) float64          degrees_north
longitude (551,) float64          degrees_east
time      (1,)   float64          minutes since 2000-01-01
```

**Orientation VERIFIED.** The HDF5 dimension scales explicitly bind
`AOD.dim1 -> /latitude` and `AOD.dim2 -> /longitude`, so the pipeline's
`grid[np.ix_(lat_mask, lon_mask)]` indexing is correct. This was checked
rather than assumed, because a transposed axis would silently corrupt every
satellite value. Note that `latitude` is **descending** (45.05 -> -9.95);
boolean masking handles this correctly.

- **Single bounding box** for all 169 granules: lat [-9.95, 45.05], lon
  [45.05, 100.05], 0.1-degree spacing.
- **Overpass times (UTC):** 05:30 x154, 06:00 x7, 06:30 x4, 07:00 x3,
  07:30 x1. 166 distinct dates from 169 granules; no duplicate (date, time)
  pairs; exactly one date carries two overpasses.
- **Attribute `Acquisition_Date` matches the filename date for all 169.**
- **Station window (+/-0.25 deg, 5 x 5 = 25 px max):** median **12** valid
  pixels, mean 14.1, and **27 granules yield zero valid pixels**. (D-004)
- **No QA, cloud or quality-flag dataset exists** in these granules, so
  "valid pixel" cannot be distinguished from "clear-sky retrieval".
- AOD values observed in range [2.6e-06, 3.0]; the [0, 5] acceptance window
  discards nothing.

### 6.3 MERRA-2

172 subsetted granules of `MERRA2_400.tavg1_2d_slv_Nx` (NASA GMAO,
VersionID 5.12.4, DOI 10.5067/VJAFPLI1CSIV).

- **Grid: 1 x 1 cell at (28.5 N, 77.5 E)** — a single point, not a collocation
  box.
- **Timestamps: 12, 13, 14 only** (11:30, 12:30, 13:30 UTC). The granule's
  own `history_L34RS` attribute confirms
  `Spatial: 77.2 77.4 28.3 28.5. Timesteps: 12 13 14`.
- Variables: `PS`, `QV10M`, `QV2M`, `SLP`, `T10M`, `T2M`, `T2MDEW`, `TQI`,
  `TQL`, `TS`, `U10M`, `V10M`.
- **`PBLH` missing in all 172 files. `PBLTOP` also missing in all 172.** The
  pipeline correctly drops the feature and reports it, but the configured
  `PBLTOP` fallback is a latent trap. (D-008)
- `V10M` missing in 2 files.
- Coverage 2024-01-01 to 2024-06-20; 10 June days absent (2024-06-21
  onwards) — the granules simply stop.

The pipeline's docstring claims values are "spatially averaged over the
Faridabad bounding box"; the data support neither the spatial average nor a
daily mean. They are **midday-window single-cell values**. (D-010)

## 7. Model artefacts

`models/random_forest_pm25.joblib` and `models/xgboost_pm25.joblib` are
**tracked in git** and were **modified in the working tree** (RF 1,841,489 ->
887,425 bytes; XGB 138,388 -> 513,232 bytes). Tracked binary model artefacts
are a reproducibility hazard: they can be deserialised without any record of
which code version or dataset produced them. The plan's required documentation
list does not include a model registry, but requires that "every experiment
must save a manifest containing Git SHA, environment and package versions,
input hashes, ... model configuration, random seed". The current
`results/legacy_chapter/metrics.json` covers configuration and seed but not a content hash
of the artefact.

Both files were regenerated by the reproduction run and are consistent with
the committed metrics. Their provenance is, however, only inferable, not
recorded.

### Recommended next step

**Phase 1 (CPCB ingestion and the Track A dataset) is the correct next
phase**, and it is more valuable than it first appears. Track A needs only the
CPCB record, which spans **9 years and 2,979 accepted daily days** — 18x more
data than the 166-row fused frame. Two of the three highest-severity defects
(D-001 lag correctness, D-013 aggregation) live in the CPCB ingestion path
that Phase 1 rebuilds, so fixing them during Phase 1 is cheaper than fixing
them afterwards. Track B's multisource question should be treated as an
open experiment with matched-date evaluation, not as a demonstration of a
result already obtained.
